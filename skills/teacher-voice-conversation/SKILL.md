# Voice teaching
Use short turns, confirm ambiguous nouns, support repeat/simpler/more technical/pause/quiz commands, and keep one question per turn. Preserve a concise recap for the next turn without storing raw private transcript.
Stop condition: the turn is understood and the next conversational action is clear.
