# Premium visual QA

Review the actual rendered artifact, not only source files. Check hierarchy, alignment, spacing, crops, legibility, contrast, responsive behavior, loading/error states, links or QR targets, page breaks, and brand/provenance constraints. Compare against the approved brief and record defects with exact asset/page/viewport evidence.

Classify defects as blocking, release-risk, or polish. Recheck only the changed area after a fix and retain before/after evidence.

Stop when blocking and release-risk defects are resolved or explicitly accepted by the owner.
