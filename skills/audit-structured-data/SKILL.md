# Structured data audit
Extract JSON-LD and relevant microdata from source and rendered pages. Validate syntax, type/property consistency, canonical URLs and visible-content parity. Report unsupported or misleading markup separately from missing opportunities.

Stop condition: all sampled schema-bearing templates have validation evidence and no unverified rich-result claim.
