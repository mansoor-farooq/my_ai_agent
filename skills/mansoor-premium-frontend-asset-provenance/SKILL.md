# Mansoor Frontend Asset Provenance
Record source, license, revision, attribution and transformations for every non-code asset. Optimize formats and loading strategy without stripping required rights metadata. Keep an owner-provided or licensed fallback for critical content; never substitute an unverified asset just to complete a visual demo.

Stop condition: asset rights, optimized output and fallback path are documented.
