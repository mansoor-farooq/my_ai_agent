# Mansoor Image Asset Provenance
Classify each asset as owner-provided, licensed reuse, public inspiration or newly generated. Preserve transparency, dimensions, color profile and required brand constraints. Record source/license or generation brief, inspect the output, and avoid copying recognizable protected artwork without authorization.

Stop condition: asset provenance, output inspection and destination are documented.
