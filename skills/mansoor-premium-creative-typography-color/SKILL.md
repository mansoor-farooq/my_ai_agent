# Premium typography and color

Define display, heading, body, label, numeric, and legal-copy roles with fallback fonts, optical sizing, line-height, tracking, and wrapping limits. Define semantic colors, contrast targets, dark/light behavior, print-safe alternatives, and color-blind-safe distinctions.

Keep brand colors and licensed fonts traceable. Validate actual rendered glyphs, punctuation, numerals, and multilingual fallback rather than relying on a design mockup alone.

Stop when the tokens are reusable across UI and exported artifacts and all required contrast/readability checks pass.
