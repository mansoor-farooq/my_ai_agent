# Materials and provenance
Prefer official curriculum indexes and openly licensed repositories. Record board, grade, subject, medium, version, licence and source URL. Link or quote minimally from all-rights-reserved books; do not scrape or redistribute a textbook corpus.
Stop condition: every material reference has a provenance and rights boundary.
