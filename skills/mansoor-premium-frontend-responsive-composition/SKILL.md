# Mansoor Responsive Composition
Compose by available constraints, not device-name branches. Define art direction, typography, media crop, interaction density and navigation behavior for compact, medium and wide spaces. Keep important content and controls present when the 3D or motion layer is disabled.

Stop condition: the experience is coherent at required widths, zoom levels and orientation changes without overflow.
