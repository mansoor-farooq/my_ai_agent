# On-page and content audit
Inspect rendered and served title, description, H1-H6 hierarchy, language, hreflang, visible text, duplicate patterns and intent alignment. Distinguish observed facts from recommendations and avoid inventing business claims.

Stop condition: every requested template has evidence-backed on-page findings or an explicit coverage gap.
