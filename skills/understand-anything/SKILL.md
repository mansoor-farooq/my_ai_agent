# Understand Anything — Codebase Knowledge & Architectural Graphs

Turn any codebase into an organized, teachable mental model and dependency graph. Ground your architectural understanding before modifying mission-critical files.

## Core Capabilities
1. **Interactive Architecture Mapping:**
   - Map entry points, service layers, data access layers, and external dependencies.
   - Trace cross-cutting concerns: Authentication, authorization, caching, state machines, and event emission.
2. **Component Call Trees & Lineage:**
   - Trace upstream caller graphs and downstream impact cones before refactoring.
   - Detect circular dependencies and high-risk coupling.
3. **Domain Entity Glossaries:**
   - Extract domain concepts (e.g. Tenants, Warehouses, Batches, Slabs, Ledgers) and their business invariants.
4. **Targeted Code Walkthroughs:**
   - Provide concise, progressive walkthroughs for onboarding and multi-file debugging.
