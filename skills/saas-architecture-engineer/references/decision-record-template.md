# SaaS decision record template

```markdown
# ADR-[number]: [decision]

## Context
[Observed constraints, current architecture, and evidence]

## Decision
[One concrete choice and its boundary]

## Tenant and authorization impact
[Tenant scope, permission checks, audit events]

## Alternatives considered
[At least one lower-complexity alternative and why it was not chosen]

## Migration and rollback
[Small deployable slices, compatibility window, rollback trigger]

## Fitness checks
[Automated checks that prevent regression]
```
