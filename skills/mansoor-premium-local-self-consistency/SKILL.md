# Local bounded consistency

Request a small fixed number of independent candidates only for high-impact ambiguity. Compare structured decisions and evidence, not stylistic prose. Prefer one grounded candidate over a majority of unsupported guesses.

Stop at the configured sample cap and report disagreement instead of hiding it.
