# Mansoor Artifact Quality
Validate the artifact itself, not only the generating command. Check format, dimensions/schema, metadata, readability/playback, reproducibility and destination path. Keep source inputs and generated outputs distinct. A missing preview or unreadable artifact is unverified, not a pass.

Stop condition: the deliverable opens or executes as intended and its evidence is recorded.
