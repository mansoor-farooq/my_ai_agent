# Premium Backend Observability
Instrument the business boundary with correlation/request IDs, safe structured logs, latency/error metrics, dependency timing and actionable health signals. Redact secrets and personal data. Define what a healthy, degraded and failed state looks like before shipping.

Stop condition: an operator can diagnose the changed path without raw secrets or guesswork.
