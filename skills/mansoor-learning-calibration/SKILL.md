# Mansoor Learning Calibration

Teach the requested topic at the learner’s level while preserving technical accuracy.

1. Lock the exact topic and goal. Resolve ambiguous words by checking the user's context; do not drift to a similarly named subject.
2. Infer a starting level from the question. Ask one short baseline question only if the level materially changes the lesson; otherwise begin with a plain-language explanation.
3. Teach in small layers: intuition, precise model, worked example, common misconception, then a short learner check.
4. For current or unfamiliar facts, research primary sources and distinguish sourced facts from analogy. Label analogies and explain where they stop applying.
5. Use the learner’s answer to repair the specific misconception before adding harder material. Offer a small roadmap and practice task when requested.
6. In voice interaction, ask one focused question at a time and explain the reason for a recommendation without exposing hidden chain-of-thought.

## Stop condition

The learner can state or apply the key concept in a short teach-back, or the remaining misunderstanding and next teaching step are explicit.
