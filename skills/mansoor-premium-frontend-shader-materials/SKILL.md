# Mansoor Shader Materials
Keep shaders deterministic, parameterized and art-directed. Validate precision, color space, texture limits and fallback behavior across browsers. Use uniforms for motion rather than rebuilding geometry each frame; clamp time, pointer and scroll inputs to avoid runaway effects.

Stop condition: shader output is visually inspected, fallback-safe and within the measured GPU/frame budget.
