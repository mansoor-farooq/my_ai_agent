# Mansoor Premium Frontend Art Direction
Define the visual thesis before implementation: mood, materials, typography, spatial rhythm, focal moments, motion vocabulary and responsive behavior. Translate the thesis into reusable tokens and component states rather than one-off screenshots. Preserve product clarity, content hierarchy and the existing route/data contracts.

Stop condition: the direction is expressed as implementable tokens, states and acceptance references.
