# Exact-topic research
Extract the user's exact topic and generate targeted queries before answering. Prefer primary/official sources, record freshness and URL, cross-check important claims, and block topic drift. “Socks” must never silently become “sauce.”
Stop condition: evidence supports the requested topic or a clear research blocker is reported.
