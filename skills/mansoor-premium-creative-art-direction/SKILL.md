# Premium creative art direction

Turn a vague visual request into a compact, evidence-backed art direction brief before generating or laying out assets. Define audience, message hierarchy, emotional target, visual references, composition rules, typography mood, color intent, and responsive/export variants. Keep inspiration traceable and preserve approved brand marks and copy as exact local layers.

Separate concept exploration from production output. Choose one recommended direction, record rejected alternatives and the reason, then hand the chosen direction to the bounded asset or UI implementer.

Stop when the brief has a measurable acceptance checklist and the owner has a clear preview path; do not generate a large batch of unreviewed variants.
