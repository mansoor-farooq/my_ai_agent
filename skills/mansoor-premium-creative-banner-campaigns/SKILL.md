# Premium banner campaigns

Build a single master composition and derive platform variants from explicit safe areas, aspect-ratio rules, crop anchors, text zones, CTA priority, and minimum readable type sizes. Keep logos, product names, prices, dates, disclaimers, and legal copy as deterministic editable layers; use generation only for original decorative artwork.

Preview every required viewport or export size, inspect the actual rendered text, and reject crops that remove the subject or CTA. Record source assets, fonts, licenses, generation provenance, and final dimensions.

Stop when every requested variant passes text legibility, brand, contrast, crop, and export checks.
