# Mansoor Owner Handoff
Return a compact contract: outcome, changed paths, evidence commands/results, remaining risks, provenance, and exact next action. Distinguish `verified`, `unverified`, `blocked` and `needs-owner-decision`. Do not claim global completion from a specialist-local check. The manager owns integration and final promotion.

Stop condition: the handoff is reproducible by the manager without rereading the entire transcript.
