# Premium Backend Release Audit
Review changed paths, contracts, migrations, secrets, permissions, observability, rollback, CI evidence and operational notes. Check that tests ran on the intended revision and that skipped environments are labeled. Recommend release, hold or staged rollout with concrete reasons.

Stop condition: the release decision is evidence-backed and reversible, or a named blocker is handed to the manager.
